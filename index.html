About ChordFlow

v1.0.0 PWA

Interactive Multi-Instrument Chord Studio & Progression Builder

🌟 Overview

ChordFlow is a professional, high-performance Web Audio Single Page Application designed for musicians, songwriters, producers, and students. It enables instant chord exploration across 7 acoustic & electric instruments, customizable voicing diagrams, intelligent chord progression building with 16 world-class formulas, custom 16-step strumming pattern sequencing, a real-time chromatic pitch tuner, and interactive music theory visualization.

🚀 Core Capabilities & Features

🎸
7 Realistic Instruments

Guitar (Standard EADGBE), Piano (88-key mapped), Ukulele (GCEA), Guitalele (ADGCEA), Violin (GDAE), Bass (EADG), and 10-Hole Diatonic Harmonica in C.

🎼
144 Interactive Chords & Voicings

Major, Minor, 7th, Maj7, m7, Diminished, Augmented, Sus2, Sus4, 6th, m6, and Add9 across all 12 chromatic root notes with multiple voicing variations.

⚡
Progression Formulas & Tempo Match

16 auto-generation formulas including Japanese City Pop / Royal Road (王道進行), Just The Two of Us (丸サ進行), J-Rock Drive, Circle of Fifths cycles, Neo-Soul, Blues, and Flamenco with style-matched tempo and strum patterns.

🥁
16-Step Strumming Sequencer

Interactive 16-subdivision rhythmic grid editor with down-strums (↓), up-strums (↑), mutes (x), and accents, accompanied by a real-time synchronized playback ticker.

🎯
Real-Time Chromatic Tuner

Microphone pitch-detection engine with high-precision YIN / autocorrelation algorithm, dynamic cent deviation meter, string selection buttons, and automatic 3-strike string advance.

🎡
Circle of Fifths & Modal Theory

Interactive 12-key Circle of Fifths with diatonic chord highlighting (I, ii, iii, IV, V, vi, vii°), borrowed modal chords, Roman numeral analysis, and scale degree formula viewer.

🎛️
Dual Modes & Audio Synthesis

Seamlessly toggle between Clean Mode (streamlined 8-chord workflow) and Advanced Mode (16-chord capacity, voicings, inversion filters, custom Low-Pass/High-Pass/Decay sound design sliders).

📄
Export & Offline PWA

Export progressions to Printable PDF Chord Charts (jsPDF), MIDI files, JSON, or copyable text. Works 100% offline with zero network latency.
